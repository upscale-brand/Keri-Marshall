Our story

Health habits that fit real life.

Chomp began with a simple idea from its founders, Dr. Keri Marshall and Kelly: make it easier and more enjoyable to bring functional benefits into everyday routines.

Keri Marshall is a naturopathic doctor and epidemiologist who has helped patients find natural solutions for more than 22 years. Chomp brings that wellness perspective to a convenient chewing-gum format.

  • Commitment to health
  • Innovation in flavors and functions
  • Quality ingredients and customer care
